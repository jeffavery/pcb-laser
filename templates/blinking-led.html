READY TO BUILD · LEARNING PROJECT 01

Your blinking light board.

A small, single-sided through-hole board that flashes one LED from a 9 V battery.

What the parts do

  • The 555 chip is the tiny clock that repeatedly turns the light on and off.
  • The two timing parts tell the chip how fast to blink.
  • The LED resistor keeps the LED safe.

BEFORE ETCHING

Three quick checks

  1. Print or frame the SVG at 100% size and confirm the board will fit your parts.
  2. Use the KiCad file if you want to inspect pad locations or change the board.
  3. This learning board uses four short insulated jumper wires on the component side. They are normal for a simple single-sided board.

IMPORTANT

Do not connect the battery yet

After soldering, inspect for solder bridges and confirm the electrolytic capacitor and LED polarity before connecting 9 V. The first hardware build should be checked with a multimeter if one is available.
